# Gatecrash

A crowd-battle game. Hold and drag to aim a cannon that fires runners up a causeway. Gates multiply the runners that pass through (×2, ×3, ×4). Barricades (from level 2) are crate stacks with a number that soak up runners until broken, and always guard a bigger gate, so you choose between going around and punching through. Your runners clash one-for-one with the enemy crowd. Whatever gets through damages the enemy tower, and you win when it falls. If enemies reach your gatehouse, they damage it.

One HTML file with no dependencies. Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder.

- **Controls:** hold and drag (or arrow keys and space) to aim and fire. Fill the gold ring, then tap it (or press G) to launch a giant.
- **Enemies:** clumps of runners, plus horned brutes with a lot of health from level 2.
- **Levels:** endless. Each layout is generated from the level number. From level 3 some gates slide side to side. Tower health is calculated from how much the layout's gates multiply a shot, so a level full of ×3 gates isn't a walkover.
- **Upgrades:** coins buy fire rate, volley (extra runners per shot) and giant charge and power.
- **Progress:** saved in the browser (level, coins, upgrades, stars).
- **New pieces** (each explained with a card when it first appears):
  - Level 2: barricade
  - Level 4: shield gate (the runner's next clash costs it nothing)
  - Level 5: windmill (sweeping blades knock out runners from both sides)
  - Level 6: giant gate (runners become giants worth 6, limited uses)
  - Level 7: pit
  - Level 8: sprint gate
  - Level 9: pusher (shoves runners off the edge)
- **Crowds:** when the field gets crowded, multiplied runners merge into bigger runners that show their combined power. Runners spread apart a little, then funnel toward the tower door once they're past every gate and hazard. Enemies and brutes drift toward your cannon.
- **Tutorial and settings:** level 1 has a guided tutorial with a hand showing each gesture. Settings cover sound, vibration, and sparks and screen shake. The game pauses when you switch apps.
- **Readability:** your runners are round with a leaf; enemies have horns, angry brows and a spiky hem, so the two sides differ in shape as well as colour.
